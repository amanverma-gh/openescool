FAQ

Questions

Is OpenEscool really free?

Yes. It's open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, so you can use, change and run it for your communities at no cost. You pay only for your server and domain, plus the normal fees of Stripe or Razorpay when members pay you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The install guide is copy and paste. After that, everything (email keys, branding, home page, upload limits, SEO) is set inside the app's Admin settings.

How many communities and members can I have?

As many as your server can handle; OpenEscool has no limits. A small VPS is fine for most creators; move to a bigger plan as you grow.

How do I get updates?

Admin settings shows when a new version is out. Back up, then run php artisan app:update on your server, or use the one-click button.

Can my members see other people's communities?

Only communities on your own install, and you decide which ones show on the home page. You can even make the home page open your main community directly.

Where do I get help?

Post questions, bugs and ideas at openescool.userjot.com.
